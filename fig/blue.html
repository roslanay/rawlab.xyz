AUTHORITY, TRUTH, AND POWER Legitimacy vacated — the image steps into the void Opens the exhibition with the foundational question: how does an image — drawn, printed, coded — come to be believed? "With God Almighty being flighty, And absent frequently, It's up to you to make it through The day — with Q.R.V." — Edward Gorey, Q.R.V. (Dogear Wryde) When ultimate authority evacuates, something moves in to fill the space. Gorey identifies the condition precisely: in the absence of the divine guarantor, the tonic takes over. The festival opens with this foundational mechanism — the way graphic languages construct reality by inhabiting the aesthetic codes of legitimacy. Typographic conventions, bureaucratic formats, pseudo-archival aesthetics, the diagrammatic authority of the scientific image: these are the visual instruments by which belief is manufactured. In the illustration tradition specifically, the line between record and invention was always thinner than in photography — which makes illustration an especially precise lens through which to examine how authority is not found but built, stroke by stroke. REPRODUCIBILITY — BETWEEN PROPAGANDA AND PROTEST The compulsive image — the crowd logic of circulation Mechanism in motion: authority constructed in section I now gets mobilised, reproduced, reversed, and weaponised. "We dance and sing like anything And laugh excessively, And never pause at all because We're stuffed with Q.R.V."
