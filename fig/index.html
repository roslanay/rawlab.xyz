When ultimate authority evacuates, something moves in to fill the space. Gorey identifies the condition precisely: in the absence of the divine guarantor, the tonic takes over. The festival opens with this foundational mechanism — the way graphic languages construct reality by inhabiting the aesthetic codes of legitimacy. Typographic conventions, bureaucratic formats, pseudo-archival aesthetics, the diagrammatic authority of the scientific image: these are the visual instruments by which belief is manufactured. In the illustration tradition specifically, the line between record and invention was always thinner than in photography — which makes illustration an especially precise lens through which to examine how authority is not found but built, stroke by stroke.
